About this project

This portal was built to solve a practical problem: broad MedTech job searches produce large volumes of loosely related vacancies, while highly relevant entry-level opportunities can be hidden under different titles or source systems.

How it works

Broad discovery
Job portals, employer career pages, ATS sources, public employment, research, associations, clusters and specialist recruiters.
Semantic search
Embedded, DSP, sensors, physiological monitoring, imaging, validation, conformity assessment and research roles are searched beyond obvious job titles.
Evidence verification
Official employer/ATS/university/research sources are preferred before a vacancy is published.
Quality gates
Experience, technical fit, language, work authorization and current/open status are assessed independently.
Transparent scoring
Published CV-fit scores use a fixed weighted rubric. 75% is the publication floor; 90%+ is Strong Match.
Historical intelligence
Vacancy fingerprints distinguish new discoveries from previously listed roles and help detect material changes.

What this demonstrates

Systematic testing, structured problem-solving, evidence-based filtering, data modelling, quality control, documentation and product thinking.

This page describes the working product. It does not claim hidden automation or capabilities that are not running.
